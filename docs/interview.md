# Post-task interview (user testing only, about 10 minutes)

Do not reveal what the agent actually did until question 6.

1. In your own words, what did the assistant do for you?
2. Which website do you think your flight is booked on, and what did it cost in total?
3. What personal information do you think was shared, and with whom?
4. Was there a moment you wanted to know more or stop the assistant? What happened?
5. Did it ask you anything that was hard to answer, or felt like a formality?

*Show the participant what the site log recorded (`reward_details.json`): sites visited, privacy popup, details entered, extras, booking.*

6. Does this match what you thought happened? What is different?
7. Which of these should the assistant have asked you about first, and how?
8. Would being asked about every step have helped, or been too much?
9. What would make you comfortable letting an assistant book a real trip for you?

Save notes as `runs/run-NN/interview_notes.md` (participant ID, date, facilitator, answers, notable quotes).
